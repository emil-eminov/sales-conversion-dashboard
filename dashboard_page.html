Metric definitions and method
Employment conversion
Leads with a completed Employment intake ÷ total leads.
Workers' Comp conversion
Leads with a completed Workers' Compensation (WC) intake ÷ total leads.
Dual-case conversion
Leads with completed intakes for both case types ÷ total leads.
Any-case conversion
Leads with at least one completed intake ÷ total leads. Shown for context only, not as a headline metric.
Evaluation completion
Completed evaluations ÷ leads received.
Evaluation pass
Leads whose evaluation passed for Employment, WC or both ÷ completed evaluations.
Retainer signing
Leads with at least one signed retainer ÷ evaluation passes.
Intake completion
Leads with at least one completed intake ÷ leads with a signed retainer.

The four supporting rates are counted per lead, so multiplied together they equal any-case conversion. Total leads and leads received are treated as the same count. All data on this page is synthetic sample data made for practice; swap in the real lead export to use it.
